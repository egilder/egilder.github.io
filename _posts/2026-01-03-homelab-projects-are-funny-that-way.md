Welp, [So I did a thing](https://egilder.github.io/blog/so-i-did-a-thing/), only to undo it shortly after. Linux gaming was fantastic, it was so smooth and worked well (albiet there was a little bit of a set up having an [Nvidia](https://wiki.archlinux.org/title/NVIDIA]) graphics card). But as soon as I was just starting to get comfortable with it and even started to customize it a little the fateful day happened. One weekend I went down to play [Horizon Forbidden West Complete Edition](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2420110/Horizon_Forbidden_West_Complete_Edition/) when the computer wouldn't POST. Naturally I blamed the graphics card (as I had just recently did driver update), but correlation does not imply causation. Through hours of troubleshooting and trial and error I had come to find that a stick of my RAM just died. But that wasn't until *after* I panic installed Windows 11 (even though I was previously on Windows 10 (RIP)).

So for now I'm on Windows 11, but have been choosing to use my MacBook Pro when I'm not gaming. I have since started to venture into the "I'm taking back my own time" with things in life, which also includes my homelabbing/projects. I have really wanted to break into containerization and was going to go down the route of Docker again when I found out about [Podman](https://podman.io/). As of right now it's merely me finding it very interesting and wishing I had a reason to put forth time into it... since my current job or even future job prospects would never need it (which is exactly what I said in the very [first post](https://egilder.github.io/blog/post-first-post/) about this little venture).

So for now, I need to figure out a far better way to not only entertain myself but also use that fun as a driving force for my career.
